How to play

Land the disc in the basket's chains in as few throws as you can. After every throw the disc stays where it lands — run, jump and climb to it, then throw again. Water, lava and the void cost a stroke.
Drag upSet power, release to throw. Drag sideways to fine-tune aim.
← →Rotate your aim
Q ERelease angle — hyzer curves left, anhyzer curves right
LoftLow, normal or high launch
1–8Choose a disc: putters near the basket, drivers for distance
WASDWalk to your disc (camera-relative) · A/D nudge the disc mid-flight
Shift · SpaceSprint · jump (hold for a higher jump)
Drag · wheelWhile walking: orbit and zoom the camera
ReliefCan't reach your disc? Take a penalty stroke and replay from your last lie
C · MFirst-person view · hide the map
RBonk a nearby player (multiplayer)
From hole 3 on, each hole adds a twist — rising water, crumbling platforms, critters that steal your disc, failing lights… The hole banner tells you which. The dotted arc previews your throw and the ring shows where it lands. The green band on the power meter is the disc's sweet spot. Clip the band or rim and the basket spits the disc back out.
